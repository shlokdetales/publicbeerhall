the vibe

Good Vibes, Great Company.

PUBLIC is built on a simple idea — a great night out shouldn't need a plan. This is your everyday neighbourhood beer hall in Versova: checkerboard tables, glasses sweating in the Bombay heat, and a kitchen that takes bar food seriously. The menu travels the country to get here — Gujarat's peanut belt, Bengal's smoked bandel, charcoal sigris from the hills, the wok-fired heat of Tangra — and lands at your table the way chakna is meant to: fast, generous, and built for a second round.

There's no dress code, no reservation book, and nobody rushing you off the table. Just cold draft poured right, straight spirits without the theatre, and a room that fills up through the evening with regulars, first dates, and the odd office crew unwinding after a long week. Grab whatever seat's free and let the night figure itself out — that's the whole idea.

Frequently Asked

Do you take reservations?

No. PUBLIC is walk-in-only — no bookings, no waitlist app. Pull up a stool when there's one free.

What are your opening hours?

Open daily from midday through late, with later hours on Friday and Saturday. Check our Instagram for the current schedule, since it can shift around holidays.

Is there a dress code?

None. Come as you are — that's the whole point.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Yes. Every dish is marked veg or non-veg on the menu, and staples like Bharuch Peanuts, Sweet Chips and Dahi Baingan are all vegetarian.

Where are you located, and is there parking?

We're in Versova, Mumbai. Street parking nearby is limited, especially on weekend nights — an auto or cab is usually the easiest way in.

Can you host a private event or large group?

Since we don't take reservations, bigger groups are handled case by case — message us on Instagram ahead of time and we'll do what we can.
